# ADR 0003 — Local diarization and cited multi-recording chat

Implement in user-requested order: review the new recording's summary against its transcript; diarization; cited multi-recording chat. The real test summary faithfully reflects its two transcript segments, has full coverage and no unsupported decisions/tasks. The product name was mistranscribed; no accuracy claim against listening to the original audio is made. Private content stays out of Git.

Diarization uses sherpa-onnx CPU, upstream distributed pyannote segmentation 3.0 ONNX and 3D-Speaker ERes2Net embeddings. Provisioning is explicit, hashes recorded locally; inference never downloads models or sends audio out. Models run sequentially with Whisper/analysis, two threads and a bounded subprocess. Speaker labels are anonymous and scoped to a recording, not biometric identity. Store original timed turns plus conservative segment assignment: mixed or overlapping speakers remain unassigned. No splitting text by invented word timings. Existing summaries/reviewed tasks must survive speaker-only backfill and failures.

Chat uses local SQLite retrieval and the existing local Ollama model, bounded context, no paid provider or outbound fallback. Responses carry validated references to immutable recording/segment IDs and timestamps. Failed or unsupported generation falls back explicitly to literal excerpts. This is retrieval augmented chat, not a claim of semantic embeddings or exhaustive recall. Source text and prior conversation are untrusted data. User can select multiple recordings; scope applies to retrieval and citations. Serialize inference requests, cap history and context, reject invalid citations and avoid storing conversation on the server in this iteration.

Roll back product code/image and disable diarization model environment variable; additive tables and original audio/transcripts remain. Preserve backup before deployment/backfill. Human multi-speaker accuracy still requires a representative real recording; one-speaker success is insufficient.
